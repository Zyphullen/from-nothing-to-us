## v8 (2 Nov 2026)

**New**
- **Explore menu**: a side menu with All the elements and a living Virtual Cell.
- **Virtual Cell**: deep zoom from the whole cell to organelles, molecules (water, sugar, ATP, lipids, DNA base pairs, proteins), glowing atoms, and inside an atom (an electron cloud around a tiny nucleus). A breadcrumb and scale bar go down to picometres. Tap any part for a short card with Read more, a certainty colour and a source. Calm and offline; it pauses if you prefer reduced motion, and it lowers detail on slower devices.
- **Tree of everything**: the Tree view is an organic tree, with roots under "Before the Big Bang?", a glowing trunk, tapering branches and leaves, and "Us" in the crown.
- **Link preview**: new image `og-preview-2026.png` for shared links.

**Better**
- **Keyboard**: Tab to any node, Enter to open.
- **Phones**: a compact top bar, the Circle opens at a readable size, and the Key chip sits above the pop-up.
- **Circle**: a wider, rounder closing arc, and the loop card no longer covers the map.
- **Text**: bigger small text and brighter greys.
- **Science wording**: Bennu ribose (Dec 2025), LUCA figures marked as estimates, the oldest bacterial fossils marked debated, the first photosynthesis made no oxygen, and brain synapse numbers made consistent.

## v7 (2 Nov 2026)

**New**
- **Circle view**: a third option next to Map and Tree (Map / Tree / Circle). The whole journey sits on one ring in stage order, with Chain Theory on an inner ring and "Is it all one loop?" in the centre.
- **The loop**: a red, dashed, glowing line joins "Us" back to "Before the Big Bang?". Both ends are unknown. Its pop-up explains related ideas (the block universe, Wheeler's participatory universe, Penrose's cycles) and is clear that no experiment has tested this yet.
- **Share links**: work in the Circle view (`#tv=circle`, and `#tv=circle&loop=1` for the loop).
- **How to use**: the first-visit card now mentions all three views.

## v6 (26 Oct 2026)

**New**
- **Chain Theory note**: the Chain Theory nodes now show a small "New idea (Oct 2026) · still being tested" badge, and Read more explains that only one number match (~1/137.036) is confirmed so far; the wider claims are still conjecture.
- **How to use**: a small first-visit card (zoom/drag, click a node, what the colours mean, Map ⇄ Tree). Reopen it any time from the "i" panel.
- **Share a node**: every pop-up has "🔗 Copy link". Links like `#n=<node>` (and `&tv=tree`) open straight to that node in the right view.
- **Suggest a fix**: each Read more ends with a link that opens a pre-filled GitHub issue for that node; there is also a general link in the "i" panel.
- **Bounty dates**: open bounties show "Last checked: 26 Oct 2026", and solved bounties can now show who solved them, when, and a source.

## v5 (26 Oct 2026)

**New**
- **Map ⇄ Tree swap**: one button switches between the left-to-right map and a new Tree view (roots at "Nothing", branches up to "Us"). Same nodes, same pop-ups, search and path highlight in both.
- **Open bounties**: every orange ("we think") and red (unknown) node gently pulses. Under "Read more" there's an "Open bounty" button showing the open question and the tests that could solve it. Global Bounty Tree link coming soon.
- **Solved = green in both views**: when science settles a question, one data change turns it green with a tick everywhere.
- **Echo-Flame 🔥**: earn a little flame for exploring (opening nodes, reading deeper, 3D views, bounties). Saved in your browser, with a save code to move progress between devices. No accounts, no personal data.
- **Soft piano music**: an original, gentle piano loop that starts quietly after your first click, with mute and a volume slider (remembered). Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0), via FreePats.
- Tidier corner controls, a new info panel and a new link preview image.
